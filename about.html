About STOPAZ

STOPAZ traces antizionism through the longer history of Jew-hatred and carries that history into education, scholarship, and public action.

Mission

Stop Antizionism is an educational, analytical, and advocacy initiative. It defines antizionism as the third era of Jew-hatred: a national form that follows the religious era of anti-Judaism and the racial era of antisemitism.

Its central claim is that Jew-hatred changes vocabulary without losing its structure. In each era, Jews are cast as violators of the values a society treats as morally sacred. STOPAZ reads today’s language of decolonization, anti-racism, and human rights against that longer history.

Areas of work

Expose the mechanism. Teach the history. Build a response.

Research

Map the genealogy of antizionist ideas, propaganda, libels, and moral inversions as they move through education, media, culture, and public life.

Education

Build curricula, certificate programs, school and university education, professional training, and public resources that make the history usable.

Guidance

Give leaders precise categories for distinguishing policy criticism from collective demonization, drawing clear boundaries, and speaking plainly.

Partnerships

Work with educators, researchers, community organizations, public leaders, and allied groups on programs, events, strategy, and public engagement.

Three eras

Religion. Race. Nation.

Three eras without erasing their differences: anti-Judaism casts the Jew through religion, antisemitism through race, and antizionism through nationhood. The continuity makes the present easier to recognize.
