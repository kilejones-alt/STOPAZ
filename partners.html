Partnerships

STOPAZ works with researchers, educators, community leaders, and allied organizations on education, research, strategy, communications, and public programs.

Partnership scope

Partnership should add a defined capability.

Education

Curriculum, educator training, student programs, workshops, public education, and learning resources.

Research

History, behavioural science, policy, law, media analysis, evidence development, and research designed for public use.

Communications

Writing, media, digital strategy, technology, branding, public outreach, and crisis communications.

Community Programs

Events, coalition work, community engagement, project delivery, fundraising, volunteer coordination, and public convenings.

Partner process

Explain the contribution. Define the work.

Prospective partners are asked to describe their organization, expertise, proposed collaboration, examples of work, and availability, and to affirm the Global Declaration as part of the partnership process.
