Leadership

STOPAZ leadership combines historical scholarship, education, executive strategy, and applied behavioural science.

Founder

Dr. Naya Lekht, PhD

Born in the former Soviet Union, Dr. Naya Lekht is a scholar, educator, and writer whose work connects Soviet antizionism, Jewish identity, Holocaust-memory suppression, and the longer history of anti-Jewish movements.

She earned a PhD in Russian Literature from UCLA and developed her three-era account after years of curriculum work on how Jew-hatred changes from anti-Judaism to antisemitism to antizionism.

Executive Director

Natasha H. Pein

Natasha H. Pein is an educator, author, speaker, and executive leader born in the former Soviet Union. Her work focuses on antizionist propaganda, curriculum, public response, media rhetoric, and Jewish identity.

She founded PIE4ALL after curriculum work in Ontario and brings lived Soviet experience, education, public speaking, and executive strategy to STOPAZ.

Senior Behaviour Scientist

Dr. Brian Cugelman, PhD

Dr. Brian Cugelman is an applied psychologist and behavioural strategist with more than two decades of work in behaviour change, digital psychology, public engagement, research, and international programs.

His experience includes United Nations work, university teaching, digital-behaviour training, and advisory work on resilience, communication, online manipulation, and evidence-based strategy.
