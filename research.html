Research

Research & Analysis

Follow the language backward: where today’s claims were formed, how they spread, and why old libels return in modern moral dress.

Research areas

Origins, language, transmission, response.

Genealogy

Trace religious, Soviet, post-colonial, nationalist, academic, and political lineages behind contemporary antizionist language.

Language and persuasion

Study recurring libels, conspiracy structures, moral inversion, demonization, and the villain-victim-rescuer script that converts ideology into moral certainty.

How ideas spread

Examine how these ideas move through schools, universities, media, professional life, culture, activism, and public policy.

Strategic response

Translate the research into terminology, diagnostic tools, educational material, leadership guidance, and strategies for resilience.

Current focus

The verdict can arrive before the evidence.

Words do more than describe. Terms such as colonial, apartheid, racist, and genocidal can settle the moral verdict before the evidence is heard.

Outputs

Research designed to be used.

Historical and analytical briefsTerminology and diagnostic toolsCurriculum and teaching materialLectures, panels, and public analysisExhibition research and visual archives
