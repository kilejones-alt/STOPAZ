Global Declaration

Antizionism is Jew-Hatred.

The Global Declaration states STOPAZ’s position plainly: antizionism is the modern ideological form of Jew-hatred and should be named, defined, and confronted as such.

Why a declaration matters

STOPAZ argues that antizionism is more than disagreement with Israeli policy. It becomes a worldview when Jews, Zionism, and the Jewish state are assigned collective guilt and recast as uniquely racist, colonial, or illegitimate through the moral vocabulary of the present.

The declaration is designed to give schools, organizations, and public bodies a shared standard while preserving a critical distinction: criticism of a government or policy is not the same thing as demonizing Jewish peoplehood or denying Jews equal collective rights.

Name the ideology

Break the ambiguity that allows collective demonization to present itself as ordinary political critique.

Create guardrails

Give educators, leaders, and staff common language for curriculum, policy, communications, and community standards.

Protect equal belonging

Affirm that Jews have the same rights to identity, history, belonging, and self-determination claimed for other peoples.

Prevent normalization

Make clear that humanitarian language does not excuse libels, collective guilt, exclusion, or demonology.

Read, sign, and endorse the Global Declaration.
