Our Vision

Vision Statement

A society with enough historical literacy to recognize the era it is in—and enough courage to confront it.

The future STOPAZ works toward

Recognition before response.

STOPAZ envisions a world in which antizionism is widely recognized through the longer history of Jew-hatred, rather than automatically filed as ordinary political disagreement.

Its vision links that recognition to free inquiry, individual dignity, constitutional order, civic trust, and the ability to see how liberation language can be converted into a rigid moral system of villains, victims, and rescuers.

Vision in practice

Make the mechanism visible.

Historical literacy

Teach the continuity and differences among anti-Judaism, antisemitism, and antizionism before the pattern becomes invisible through new vocabulary.

Defend open societies

Treat free inquiry, individual dignity, equal standards, and moral agency as part of the response—not as collateral concerns.

Prepare public leaders

Give educators, journalists, policymakers, and community leaders language that can withstand ideological pressure and public confusion.

Restore intellectual honesty

Build a public discourse where humanitarian language cannot function as automatic moral cover for collective guilt, exclusion, or demonization.
