World Symposium Against Antizionism

The 2026 World Symposium Against Antizionism brought scholarship, education, law, media, advocacy, and public leadership into one program focused on the third era of Jew-hatred.

WSAA 2026

Eight panels and keynotes. One argument carried across them.

The archive includes panel recordings and keynote presentations, media coverage from outlets including the Times of Israel, National Post, JNS, The Hub, and Canadaland, and information about participation and sponsorship for 2027.

Genealogy & Entrenchment

What antizionism is, how Soviet and later ideologies developed, and how those ideas moved into modern public life.

Law & Education

Legal boundaries, academic integrity, curriculum, the Cycle of Libels, the role of token Jews, and what follows when the problem is named badly.

Media & Weaponized Language

Disinformation, alternative media, public communication, and the language that turns Israel and Zionism into ready-made moral villains.

Leadership & Civilizational Stakes

Islamic antizionism, next-generation advocates, civic resilience, and the argument that antizionism is not only a Jewish problem but a wider challenge to open societies.
